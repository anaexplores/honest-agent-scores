## Notes

- **Availability risk.** The Terminal-Bench 4.0 trials stay with Harbor Hub. On 2026-10-12, 11 of the 21 leaderboard submissions were already private there, and the remaining 10 can become private at any time. If `fetch_tb40_trials.py` fails or the checksum differs, the published results in `results/` cannot be recomputed from scratch; the aggregate outputs remain.
- **Which trials count.** A trial counts as a success when reward >= 1. Errored trials (124 of 3,300, of which 49 timeouts) have no reward and count as failures, as on the leaderboard. This penalises pass^5 more than pass@1.
- **Repeat independence.** The `attempt` field is 1 for every trial, so whether the 5 trials of a task ran independently cannot be checked from the data.
- **Benchmark version.** All 10 jobs report the same task-set hash (`sha256:39d9f44b...`). That this is exactly the version Epoch reviewed (4.0.0) is inferred from the leaderboard and the dates, not stated by a source.
- **Selection.** Only public submissions could be used; if public and private submissions differ systematically, the sample is biased.
- **Terminal-Bench 2.0: corrected download (2026-10-13).** The first published version of `data/tb20_trials.csv` (retrieved 2026-08-04) was defective in two ways, and every TB 2.0 result was recomputed:
  - *Wrong pages.* For the 17 requests at offsets 3,500-5,199 the Hugging Face datasets-server returned the page 600 rows further on. The row count still matched, so the old check missed it: rows 3,500-5,199 of the old file were a copy of source rows 4,100-5,799, and the true source rows 3,500-4,099 (mostly `caffe-cifar-10`) were missing. This is why one task seemed "missing almost everywhere" and the selection rule for Q5 was relaxed (see [docs/ANALYSIS_PLAN.md](docs/ANALYSIS_PLAN.md)). `scripts/fetch_tb20_trials.py` now checks that every page holds exactly the requested row indices (and retries otherwise) and that the indices are exactly 0 to 52,103. The source row index is kept in the `source_row` column.
  - *Duplicated trials in the source.* In 11 configurations (all served through together_ai or fireworks_ai) the dataset lists each trial twice under the same `trial_name`; the second copy has no `trial_id` and no `started_at`. The two copies agree on the reward in every case. Example: source rows 551-556 and 563-568 (terminus-2 with openai/gpt-oss-120b@together_ai, task adaptive-rejection-sampler). Without deduplication these configurations seem to have about 10 trials per task, of which the second half repeats the first, so "5 random trials" partly sampled the same trial twice. A trial is identified by (agent, model, trial_name); 4,952 rows were removed, keeping the copy with a `trial_id`. Per configuration: terminus-2 with openai/gpt-oss-20b@together_ai 466, Qwen/Qwen3-Coder-480B-A35B-Instruct-FP8@together_ai 464, openai/gpt-oss-120b@together_ai 462, moonshotai/Kimi-K2-Instruct-0905@together_ai 461, accounts/fireworks/models/glm-4p6@fireworks_ai 452, accounts/fireworks/models/minimax-m2@fireworks_ai 451, moonshotai/Kimi-K2-Thinking@together_ai 446; mini-swe-agent with openai/gpt-oss-20b@together_ai 443, openai/gpt-oss-120b@together_ai 442; openhands with Qwen/Qwen3-Coder-480B-A35B-Instruct-FP8@together_ai 435, moonshotai/Kimi-K2-Instruct-0905@together_ai 430. After deduplication every `trial_name` is unique. `trial_id` is empty for about half of the remaining trials (for most trials of most configurations outside these 11), so `trial_name` is the identifier.
  - *Effect on the Q5 data.* Old: 63 configurations x 88 tasks (relaxed rule). New, original rule: 60 configurations x 89 tasks; relaxed rule as a check: 75 configurations x 78 tasks.
